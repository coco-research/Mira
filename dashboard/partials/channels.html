Per-channel differentiation is a monetization requirement, not a nicety. YouTube's policy explicitly targets "content that's easily replicable at scale" and "mass-produced content using a similar or unoriginal template across multiple channels" — the exact pattern a fleet of AI channels can fall into. Each Mira channel must maintain a distinct niche, brand identity, voice, and editorial angle so no two channels read as the same template side-by-side. AI Tools (discovery angle, punchy shorts), Deep Dives (honest long-form tests), and Quick Wins (single-tip productivity, auto volume) are intentionally different formats, tones, visual languages, and cadences. Enforcement is network-level: one demonetized channel can endanger the rest if they appear clearly related and templated. Keep them genuinely separate.
